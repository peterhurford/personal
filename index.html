Peter Wildeford

Peter Wildeford is the Head of Policy at The AI Policy Network, an organization building bipartisan support for policies that prepare America for AI superintelligence.

He has spoken about AI on Good Morning America, the Daily Show, NPR, NBC's Meet the Press, CNN, NewsMax, TIME, BBC, Politico, TechCrunch, The Information, The Dispatch, and MTS. He is active on Substack and Twitter.

Previously, he co-founded the Institute for AI Policy and Strategy and before that he was a data scientist and software engineer for five years. He is also a top-20 forecaster on Metaculus and has placed highly in multiple forecasting tournaments.

ADFGVX Cipher Simulator

A WWI German field cipher: Polybius fractionation over a 6×6 square, then keyed columnar transposition.

Show the current Polybius square

Each letter/digit maps to a row label + column label drawn from A D F G V X.

Letters A–Z and digits 0–9 are kept; everything else is dropped. Decryption expects only the letters A D F G V X.
